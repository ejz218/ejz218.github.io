Class I PHI is an operational distinction within Class I. PHI is health information Lehigh holds as a covered entity, covered health care component, or business associate, which requires a Business Associate Agreement with any vendor that touches it. Two categories are excluded: student health and counseling records, which are FERPA education records and Class II, and employment records held by HR in Lehigh's role as employer, which HIPAA excludes from the PHI definition and which are also Class II.
